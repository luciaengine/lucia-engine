Local reflection probes

Add a Reflection Probe to capture nearby scene lighting for image-based reflections. The entity transform positions and orients its influence volume; scale sets its extent. Choose a box or ellipsoid influence and adjust Blend Distance for a soft transition at its boundary.

Place and tune a probe

  1. Position the probe inside the space it represents and fit the influence to that space.
  2. Adjust the capture origin offsets when the influence center is a poor viewpoint.
  3. Use projection scale and offset to fit the room or reflecting enclosure independently of the influence volume.
  4. Set capture resolution, cull mask, and maximum distance for the scene content the capture needs.

Overlapping volumes

The renderer supports two selected local probes per world. Priority controls selection when more probes compete. Use overlapping influence volumes and blend distance to soften transitions. Keep the number of simultaneously relevant probes small; adding more probes does not increase this selection limit.

Local reflection probes approximate the surrounding environment. Use planar reflections for a flat mirror and screen-space reflections for reflections from currently visible scene content.
