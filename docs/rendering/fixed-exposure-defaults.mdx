---
title: Fixed exposure defaults
description: Matching Scene View and game camera exposure in new scenes.
---

New game cameras start with **Fixed** exposure at EV100 15.4 and zero exposure compensation. The **Day and Night** preset is selected by default. Under a physical sky, it brightens twilight and night according to the sun’s elevation. Choose **Constant** to keep the base exposure at every hour. The camera stores the equivalent linear multiplier. The Scene View defaults to **Game Camera**, which follows the active camera's exposure settings.

Saved camera values and Scene View preferences remain unchanged. A saved custom exposure can still make Scene View and Game View look different.
