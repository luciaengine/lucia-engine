---
title: Fixed exposure defaults
description: Matching Scene View and game camera exposure in new scenes.
---

Scene View and new game cameras start with **Fixed** exposure at EV100 15.4 and zero exposure compensation. The camera stores the equivalent linear multiplier. Auto exposure and Scene View adaptation delay are opt-in.

Saved camera values and Scene View preferences remain unchanged. A saved custom exposure can still make Scene View and Game View look different.
